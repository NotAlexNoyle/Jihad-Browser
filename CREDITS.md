# Credits

## Personas bundled with Jihad Browser

Jihad Browser comes with fifteen Personas (render/goanna/personas/), offered in about:addons →
Personas and about:personas.

**Five drawn for Jihad Browser** — Bubble Lagoon, Cloud Nine, Meadow Morning, Aurora Silk and
Lime Fizz — by `render/goanna/personas/make-personas.py`, from gradients and shapes; MPL-2.0 like
the rest of the engine-side code.

**Ten made by others**, redistributed under their Creative Commons licences. Each was published on
addons.mozilla.org by its author under the licence shown (checked 2026-10-08; the survey and the
reasons others were left out are in `docs/research/persona-licences.md`). The picture is the one in
the author's own package, taken unchanged; the browser only reads it as a Persona. Under CC BY-SA
the pictures stay under CC BY-SA.
