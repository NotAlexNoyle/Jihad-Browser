The finger round, 2026-10-24, the first finding: a double tap on desktop Wikipedia zooms to 6.2x and the card goes grey
======================================================================================================================

Emulated finger (tools/device/finger), the Enyo card, the upstart daemon (GPU path, express lane), the page styled
(30 s after the launch), one `finger dtap 384 600`:

  [jihad-bs] smartZoom at 559,752 -> block [550,734 654,770]
  [jihad-bs] setZoomAndScroll zoom=6.1935 (was 0.6882) scroll=3344,4186
  [jihad-bs] painted shmid=0x1604000b bytes=0 (768x1224 top=4045 band=4186 left=3344) mZoom=6.1935 ... ms=1565 rows new=0 copied=0
  [jihad-bs] painted shmid=0x1604000b bytes=0 ... ms=36   (x4 more, then bytes=1, bytes=1)

The card showed the infobox cell "Introductory price" at 6.2x one second after the tap, then grey (shots p1, p8 of the
session, deleted after judging). A second double tap: `smartZoom at 602,759: no block`, no frames: no way back.

Two causes:
1. The block: GoannaRenderPage::SmartZoomBlockAt took the first block of 60 px or more up from the tapped element — a
   104 px wide table cell. The adapter fits the block's width to the card: 768 / (104 + 20) = 6.19.
2. The cap: the adapter caps a zoom at the page's maximum-scale when a meta viewport message came, else at its
   kMaxZoom 4.0. Desktop Wikipedia declares `width=1120` without a maximum, and the engine's ViewportInfo answers
   Gecko's default maximum, 10, which the daemon forwarded:
     [jihad-bs] metaViewport -> card: initial=0.6857 min=0.1000 max=10.0000 1120x1374 userScalable=1
   so the card let a double tap reach 6.2 and would let a pinch reach 10.

Fixes (daemon, GoannaRenderPage.cpp): a block must be at least a third of the card's width minus the adapter's
margin (the infobox table, the column), and a viewport tag that names no maximum-scale is reported with 4.0.
The GPU path's empty frames at 6.2x stay open as a question for zooms the cap still allows (checked next with a
pinch to the cap).

The round's findings after that, same day (the emulated finger, the Enyo card, the GPU path)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Steady drag, jihad-long-static.html, six strokes of 750 px in 3 s: the fixed header stayed put in every shot
(two mid-stroke), the paragraphs ran 12, 24, 35, 46, 57, 68, 79 with no repeat, no gap, no blank; mid-stroke
the page was 3 paragraphs on (it tracks the finger). PASS (T-502's scroll row).

Flick (160 ms, 600 px, coasting ~2000 px): the card arrives within ~250 ms; the engine paints only at the
final position (one `painted` top per flick, 4-7 frames), so for ~0.5 s after the finger lifts the lower
part of the card is blank and the fixed header is gone (shots at 0, 250, 500 ms after seven flicks: blank
from paragraph 133/161 down, header missing until the 750 ms shot). At rest everything is right. OPEN:
a frame for the destination during the coast (the GPU floor work, T-527).

Double tap on desktop Wikipedia: (1) the smart zoom fitted a 104 px cell (6.2x) and the engine's default
maximum-scale of 10 passed the adapter — fixed in the daemon (the block rule: a third of the card; a tag
without maximum-scale reports 4.0). (2) The second double tap found "no block" at a point inside the
infobox: on the GPU path the card's position lives in the engine's scroll and the compositor's shift, not
the render pan the mapping read — fixed (docOffset/gpuAlignForHitTest: the engine is scrolled to the
card's position before a discrete hit test; a zoomed-in page with no block under the finger answers the
page's width, which the adapter clamps to the fit). Verified: tap on a scrolled fit-zoom page opens the
tapped link (Palm Pre 2); double tap in to 2.33, double tap out to 0.69. (3) At rest the zoomed frame lost
its right third at 2.33, two thirds at 2.71, all of it from 2.98: the resolution following the zoom (T-504)
shrank the displayport — Gecko's GetDisplayPortFromMarginsData takes the root's base in layout device px
and divides base + margins by the resolution, so the 1116 px scroll-port base became 479 CSS px and the
displayport ended 550 px from the engine's scroll, while the card's view starts 531 px over (the engine has
no sideways range on a page laid out at the card's width). With the resolution off (JIHAD_GPU_NO_RES=1)
every zoom painted in full; with side margins of two widths (JIHAD_GPU_DP_X=2) the tiles reached the
frame's edge. Fix in the engine (jihad_layout_set_displayport2: the base is what the card shows, at the
compositor's shift, times the resolution; the margins times the resolution) and the daemon (the shift
passed, the displayport set again when the shift moves past the re-anchor margin). Build pending.

The zoom, after the fixes (same day, evening)

Engine c79d16f8 (patch 0059) and daemon 7c29035a on all three variants, dev copies (the originals kept
