T-561 — the frame sampler (cavekit-finger-testing.md R2), 2026-11-03
=====================================================================

The instrument: tools/device/finger/fbstream.c (the sampler, on the device), FINGER_TRACE in finger.c (the finger's
scans), tools/host/finger-stream.py (the stream tool), tools/device/finger/stream-pan.sh (the round).

The framebuffer (probed on the device, the Enyo card open on the long page):
  fb0 and fb1: virtual_size 1024,2304, 32 bpp, stride 4096 — three pages of 768 rows; fb1 is msmfb40_30001 (the
  card's content; fb0 the system chrome). The pan offset (/sys/class/graphics/fb1/pan, FBIOGET_VSCREENINFO yoffset)
  is 0,768 at rest and cycles 0 / 768 / 1536 under a drag: a flip per frame presented. dd of one page: 0.03 s to
  /dev/null, 0.11 s to /tmp (the tmpfs write is the rest). /tmp is a 40 MB tmpfs (34 MB free).
  Orientation (the page pulled and looked at): the fb is the panel's landscape frame; the portrait screen is the fb
  rotated −90°: portrait row y = fb column x, portrait column x = 767 − fb row y. So a portrait-row signature is a hash
  DOWN an fb column, and the sampler reads fb rows (4 KB each, contiguous) and folds each pixel into its column.

The sampler's cost (one drag down and one up on the long page, 5 s, the job's own daemon; cpu = this process's
user+system over the 5 s):
  stride 4, mmap, 15/s:  read median 13.3 ms, max 67 ms; 11.6 % of a core; 115 flips
  stride 8, mmap, 15/s:  read median  2.7 ms, max 14 ms;  5.6 % of a core; 127 flips   <- the default
  stride 4, pread, 15/s: read median 21.3 ms, max 76 ms; 18.6 % of a core
  stride 2, mmap, 20/s:  read median 16.6 ms, max 115 ms; 18.8 % of a core
  The pan poll (1 ms asked) runs at 375-540 a second: the device's nanosleep is ~2 ms. The flips under the two
  1 s drags: ~115-127, i.e. the card presents ~50-60 frames a second while the finger moves (the daemon publishes
  ~19 on this page: the card's own scroll animation presents between the daemon's frames).
  A read the card flipped under (3 of 75 at stride 8, 16 of 75 at stride 4) is taken again since: the page read may
  be the next frame's canvas.
  The device's libc is 2.8: the tools need GLIBC_2.4 symbols only (no clock_gettime — gettimeofday, the daemon's
  timeline clock, everywhere; strtol not sscanf).

What the first streams said (FBS1: exact signatures only; the tool at that state):
  Wikipedia: the shown scroll tracks the finger (the drag of 650 rows in 1 s showed 617-643 rows by the last sample
  with the finger down, 84-91 ms median lag, -33..-39 rows median error); the long page the same (617-650 rows,
  75 ms). A frame composed afresh at the fit zoom (0.6882) has every row's hash different from the last frame's
  (the bilinear phase: the band frames copy rows exactly, a main-lane frame or a band's new rows do not), so the
  exact match found no shift at the first frame of each drag and voted a wrong, backwards shift (-65..-130) on a few
  frames: hence the profile (the sum of r+g+b down the sampled columns) in FBS2 and the correlation search.
  Wikipedia's fixed sidebars stand in columns: a blank content row under them has the sidebar's signature and never
  "moves", which the stale detector read as a stale band (132 of them in a clean run): hence the content columns
  (-c) of FBS3, the columns outside getting a signature of their own. The long page's rows 82-138 (the fixed header,
  56 px at zoom 1) changed in 42 % of the samples: the card's scroll indicator at the right edge (portrait column
  767: fcfcfc at rest, c8c8c8 under the finger), so the long page's content columns are 0-756. The rows that never
  changed over 523 samples on either page: 0-82 (the status bar's black and the card's chrome).
