T-561 — the frame sampler (cavekit-finger-testing.md R2), 2026-11-03
=====================================================================

The instrument: tools/device/finger/fbstream.c (the sampler, on the device), FINGER_TRACE in finger.c (the finger's
scans), tools/host/finger-stream.py (the stream tool), tools/device/finger/stream-pan.sh (the round).

The framebuffer (probed on the device, the Enyo card open on the long page):
  fb0 and fb1: virtual_size 1024,2304, 32 bpp, stride 4096 — three pages of 768 rows; fb1 is msmfb40_30001 (the
  card's content; fb0 the system chrome). The pan offset (/sys/class/graphics/fb1/pan, FBIOGET_VSCREENINFO yoffset)
  is 0,768 at rest and cycles 0 / 768 / 1536 under a drag: a flip per frame presented. dd of one page: 0.03 s to
  /dev/null, 0.11 s to /tmp (the tmpfs write is the rest). /tmp is a 40 MB tmpfs (34 MB free).
  Orientation (the page pulled and looked at): the fb is the panel's landscape frame; the portrait screen is the fb
  rotated −90°: portrait row y = fb column x, portrait column x = 767 − fb row y. So a portrait-row signature is a hash
  DOWN an fb column, and the sampler reads fb rows (4 KB each, contiguous) and folds each pixel into its column.

The sampler's cost (one drag down and one up on the long page, 5 s, the job's own daemon; cpu = this process's
user+system over the 5 s):
  stride 4, mmap, 15/s:  read median 13.3 ms, max 67 ms; 11.6 % of a core; 115 flips
  stride 8, mmap, 15/s:  read median  2.7 ms, max 14 ms;  5.6 % of a core; 127 flips   <- the default
  stride 4, pread, 15/s: read median 21.3 ms, max 76 ms; 18.6 % of a core
  stride 2, mmap, 20/s:  read median 16.6 ms, max 115 ms; 18.8 % of a core
  The pan poll (1 ms asked) runs at 375-540 a second: the device's nanosleep is ~2 ms. The flips under the two
  1 s drags: ~115-127, i.e. the card presents ~50-60 frames a second while the finger moves (the daemon publishes
  ~19 on this page: the card's own scroll animation presents between the daemon's frames).
  A read the card flipped under (3 of 75 at stride 8, 16 of 75 at stride 4) is taken again since: the page read may
  be the next frame's canvas.
  The device's libc is 2.8: the tools need GLIBC_2.4 symbols only (no clock_gettime — gettimeofday, the daemon's
  timeline clock, everywhere; strtol not sscanf).

T-568 — the band under the fixed header: THE CARD'S HOLD FILL UNDER A FRAME THAT LEADS THE CARD.
  Where it is: render/adapter/BrowserAdapter.cpp, jihadHoldStuck (T-544, 2026-10-26). Between two frames of a pan
  the card draws the frame it holds moved by the scroll since (dY = the frame's view top - the card's scroll); each
  fixed/stuck rect of the daemon's hold set is drawn where the frame drew it, and the rows its MOVED copy covers
  outside the rect's own place are filled with ONE colour — the frame's pixel just past the rect on that side
  (jihadHoldFill: "a strip in one colour"). With dY > 0 (the frame AHEAD of the card's displayed scroll) the moved
  header's rows below the rect's place, [bottom, bottom + dY), are painted in the colour of the frame's pixel at
  (mid, bottom): the row just under the header — yellow when the highlighted block is there, the page background
  otherwise. Exactly the band: one shift tall, one colour, directly under the header, the rows below right.
  Why the frame leads: T-527's express prediction (BrowserPageGoanna.cpp xStart, JIHAD_GPU_EXPRESS_PREDICT_MS,
  default 80): "while the card coasts, the frame is composed where the card will be when it is shown (its speed
  times 80 ms: the express frame's usual age at the card's blit)" — measured on 2026-10-25. The stream measures the
  frame's age at the card's flip today at 14-18 ms median (publish to flip, p90 25-29): the lead of velocity x 80 ms
  (0.65 px/ms x 80 = 52 rows, capped at the overscan's 141) overshoots by ~40 rows, so the frame arrives ahead of
  the card's displayed position and the hold fills the lead. Evidence from the card itself (the adapter's paint log
  with a fresh web app manager, two drags on the long page): "[hold] 1 rects, moved 0,22 / 0,45 / 0,25 / 0,20 /
  0,23 / 0,18 / 0,37 / 0,17 / 0,19 / 0,18 / 0,43 ..." — the frame drawn 17-45 rows AHEAD of the card in most paints
  of a drag (and behind, -19..-26, in a few: the card ahead, no fill below); at the drag's end 0,41 five times (the
  frame settled ahead of the card), then -26..-84 (the card's scroller overshooting past the lifted finger). The
  [hp-pg] lines agree: the frame's view top (rY + 141) 283, 296, 321, 337, 362, 385 against the card's scroll 240..403.
  The same fill runs BESIDE Wikipedia's held sidebars (columns): a strip of the pixel past the sidebar's edge, which
  the stream's -c 170-600 leaves out; the owner's 2026-10-26 "the sidebars jitter and lag" was T-544's reason.
  The fix is the owner's choice (the build site's T-568 lists them): (a) the card draws the rows under a held rect from
  the previous frame it holds (its other offscreen: the content for those page rows lies below that frame's header)
  instead of one colour — exact content, the prediction kept; (b) the card paints a frame that leads it unshifted
  (the content then leads the finger by the prediction's error, no fill below; the card's own smoothing between
  frames stops only while it lags); (c) the prediction retuned from the stream's number (PREDICT_MS 80 -> ~20: the
  band shrinks to the jitter, a few rows, and does not vanish); (d) no prediction when the frame has held parts (the
  band gone, the tracking lag under a fixed header or beside a sidebar grows by the lead); (e) the daemon ships what
  lay beneath each held rect (it keeps it for its own dress) for the card to draw — the most room, the most change.
  The quick tests below (PREDICT_MS 20, 0, and the hold off) size (c) and (d) by the stream.
