T-505 item 4 — the band frames composed on the CPU from the tiles, the layers painted at the zoom's resolution
=============================================================================================================
2026-11-04. The owner's choice of 2026-11-04 (the "3" to T-567's structural fix): T-505 item 4 with the layers
painted at the zoom's resolution — the item-4 scale choice (b).

The build
---------
Engine (the fork's commit after 928649199e, CompositorBridgeParent.cpp, TiledContentHost.h): JIHAD_GPU_CPU_BANDS=1.
In JihadComposeAndRead's band path (a scroll-only frame, or one whose change fits the band pass; full-width bands
only, no column chunks, not a verify frame), before the GPU band pass: the tree is prepared as a composite prepares
it (the shadow properties, the async transforms at this frame's scroll, the parts hidden for a frame without them,
the visible regions and the effective transforms at the frame's world), then the leaves under the band rows are
collected in paint order with their scissors (CalculateScissorRect from the root's clip down, as ContainerPrepare).
Every one must be a tiled painted layer (its compositable a TiledContentHost, no low-precision tiles, no fade-in, the
buffer at resolution 1, every tile under the bands a BufferTextureHost of B8G8R8A8 or B8G8R8X8 without component
alpha) or a colour layer, drawn "over" at full opacity, no mask (own or ancestors'), under no container with a mask,
a blend mode, a 3D context or an isolated group, with a 2D effective transform of scale 1 (|m11-1|, |m22-1|, |m12|,
|m21| < 1e-3) and a translation within 0.02 px of whole pixels. Then the kept rows are shifted (JihadShiftRowsNow,
as the GPU pass does), each band's rows cleared transparent (BeginFrame's clear), each leaf drawn: a tile's rows
copied from its shared-memory buffer (memcpy for X8; a premultiplied "over" for A8) within the valid region, the
visible region (its bounds when the paint resamples) and the scissor; a colour layer's bounds filled; the alpha byte
then 0xFF as the read forces it. The composite's bookkeeping follows: the next frame asked when an animation moved,
DidComposite to the content side (its paints wait for it). The request count, the diff baseline, the fixed rows and
the parts' rects are handled by the band path's common tail as before. Anything else under a band sends the frame to
the GPU band pass as before, the reason counted: the log's "cpu bands:" line every 20 band frames, and the
"compositor thread: cpu band frame" lines (prep, shift, draw ms; leaves, tiles, rects).

Daemon (GoannaRenderPage.cpp): JIHAD_GPU_RES_ZOOM=1. GpuResolutionFor returns the zoom below 1.1 too (within 1 % of
1: 1; at least 0.1), so SetGpuViewport sets the pres shell's resolution to the zoom at every settled zoom and the
tiles' pixels land one to one on the frame (the world transform's scale times layout's post-scale of 1/resolution
= 1). The price: a full repaint of the view at each settled zoom (the critical displayport first, as a zoom in), and
the fit zoom's glyphs and images resampled by the paint rather than by the GPU (T-504's round 183 measured 3-5 fps
lost on the scripted pan at the fit zoom when every frame was read back from the GPU; this build reads none of the
band frames back).

Both off by default until measured (below); the switches documented in tools/device/t502/README.md.

The review (Codex, codex-cli 0.166.0, the diffs and the engine's sources; findings verified in the code):
  F1 bug   tile buffers race the content side: TiledContentClient's tiles carry IMMEDIATE_UPLOAD, BufferTextureHost::
           MaybeUpload uploads at the transaction and ReadUnlocks, TileClient::GetBackBuffer re-uses an unlocked
           front buffer in place. FIXED: under JIHAD_GPU_CPU_BANDS the lock stays after the upload (the tile is
           double-buffered on the content side until the host drops it); the CPU path reads only tiles whose lock is
           held.
  F2 bug   a container with an intermediate surface (opacity under 1 over a group, a mask) has surface-relative child
           transforms, scissors and opacities. FIXED: such a container, like one with a blend mode, a 3D context or an
           isolated group, sends the frame to the GPU when its own bounds meet a band, else its subtree is skipped.
  F3 bug   the clear colour: the window target clears opaque white (CompositorOGL's BeginFrame), the CPU bands cleared
           transparent black. FIXED: white.
  F4 bug   the animations' clock: the CPU prep sampled at Now() while the next JihadDiffNow sampled at the scheduler's
           last compose time (older), a rewind. FIXED: the scheduler notes the CPU frame as a compose
           (JihadNoteCompose) and the prep samples at that time, as ForceComposeToTarget does.
  F5 likely a pinch below 1.1 repaints at each step a frame gets out of (the zoom hold releases on the first frame
           at the new zoom, not on a settled zoom). NOT FIXED (the daemon's switch; documented in the t502 README).
  F6 bug   T-527's zoom-back and overview gates compare the destination's resolution with 1: a zoom back to a fit
           zoom under 0.5 keeps no frame and xUnderHalf waits for the repaint. NOT FIXED (the daemon's switch;
           documented).
  F7 bug   the tolerances: a scale within 1e-3 of 1 over 1024 px is a pixel off at the edge. FIXED: 2e-5 (float
           noise), the translation within 0.01 px of whole pixels, the tiled buffer's resolution exactly 1.
  F8 likely PaintWillResample read from the host's cached flag; the GPU refreshes it from Layer::MayResample at every
           composite. FIXED: MayResample.
  F9 risk  the draw dereferenced every visible tile's host before clipping to the band, the collect having checked
           only the tiles under the bands. FIXED: the region is cut to the band first; a non-buffer host skipped.
  F10 risk image and canvas layers off the bands: a composite's traversal keeps their timed frames and notifications
           going, and the composite-until time renews the request. FIXED: a tree with an image or canvas layer stays
           on the GPU; the composite-until renewal copied from CompositeToTarget's tail.

What it says
------------
1. The CPU bands at a scale of 1 are exact and a gain: on the long page the band frame's compositor time 22.0 ->
   4.7 ms (the 13.6 ms read gone), the express frames made in 14 ms median against 31, p90 41 against 57, the
   rate 18.2 -> 19.5 fps flowing (the nights before 19.2-19.4: the ceiling is the card's positions, ~24 a second),
   the shown scroll's worst lag 294 -> 131 ms and its worst error 169 -> 52 px; the verify 0 wrong.
2. The layers painted at the zoom's resolution (the owner's choice (b)) lose on desktop Wikipedia: the main
   thread's layer paints under the drags sum 5.1-5.3 s against 3.2-3.9 s (median 102-114 ms against 77-84, p90
   508-605 against 227-246, max 1027-1120 against 310-501) — round 183's finding of T-504 (the fit zoom's paint
   resamples every glyph and image on the CPU) stands, and the recommendation's premise (fewer pixels, cheaper
   paints) was wrong. The express lane then waits for a slot behind main-lane jobs holding one (the "no slot"
   ticks 166-232 against 78-105), the grey band at the bottom shows in 21-35 samples against 14, the express
   frames' p90 198-285 against 177-187, with the CPU bands composing 105-145 of 160-180 band frames in 20 ms
   (the compositor's share of a Wikipedia band frame 27.8-31.0 -> 20.5, the GPU read gone) — a gain swallowed
   by the paints. The rate flowing 9.6 / 10.2 / 10.4 / 9.8 (c / cb / rz / cbrz) is noise around 10.
3. The band frame's remaining cost on the CPU is outside the compositor: the long page's express frame is made
   in 14 ms of which the compositor's 4.7; Wikipedia's "all" 20.5 ms holds 15 ms of the job's entry and parts
   prep beside the 5.3 ms of prep and draw.
4. What would give Wikipedia the long page's gain without the paints' price: the tiles at resolution 1 scaled
   down on the CPU into the bands (item 4's scale choice (a): a bilinear downscale of ~80k output pixels a frame,
   NEON), the paints as they are. Not built: the owner's call.
