The slow ones: a whole 1224-row frame read and swizzled on one core with the second unplugged after an
idle pause ("cpu1 off" in the express frame's clocks; 2 per run) while the page's script runs: the read
170 ms and the CPU swizzle 150 ms where 26 and 7 are usual (r335). In r334 the first tap's frame also
waited 144 ms behind a main-lane frame at the old zoom: a zoom's express frame now gives up main-lane
frames at the old zoom that were not composed yet (it did not happen again in these runs).
The old pass mode (the swizzle on the GPU, the window target a texture) kept every tap under 190 ms but
samples the frame target during band passes (wrong band frames) and slows a simple page's pans; a GPU
swizzle through a copy texture was worse (the copy waits for the composite, 110-358 ms). Not kept.
The scripted tap calls smartZoom through the card's test hook and skips the touch path, where Palm's
governor ramps the CPU on a touch (ondemandtcl's tickle): a finger's double tap gets that ramp. Whether the
second core is up for a finger's first tap after a pause is for the owner's finger round (T-514). The
CPU's hotplug and governor are not written by the daemon (a governor write once wedged the bench).
