Bugs found and fixed on the way: wants waited on main jobs composed but unpolled (r241: the main thread
sat in the script; peek added, engine 0048); a main frame at the old zoom ended the card's wait (r254);
a frame position the engine clamps never "served" the card, so the express lane redrew it after every
main frame (r258 duplicates) - the card's commands are tagged in arrival order and the main lane serves
the card once its frame took up the newest; commands that arrive before the page exists (r259) are
why the tag is the proxy's, not a count; a stale load-time want framed before the main lane's first
frame (r246) - the main lane's first frame of a geometry or document comes first.
