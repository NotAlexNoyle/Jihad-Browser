T-517, the compositor job's floor on desktop Wikipedia's pan: rounds 314-320, 2026-10-19, CE 3.1.0 TouchPad.
pan-run.sh on en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HP_TouchPad (inject pan 0 -> 3000 px in 10 s, PAN_DELAY=15), daemon env
JIHAD_GPU_COMPOSITOR=1 JIHAD_GPU_EXPRESS=1 JIHAD_GPU_TIMELINE=1. PAN_TOP=1 (new) samples the processes'
and the daemon's threads' CPU over 4 s of the pan.

Where a pan frame goes (the band frames' own breakdown, r314/r315/r318)
  band frames with no column chunks    18-27 ms: compose 4.5-6.6 (one or two passes), shift 2.6-9.7, read 3.3-8.7
  band frames with column chunks       40-46 ms: compose 22-28 (3.3-3.8 chunk passes besides the bands), read 8-10
  most of the pan's frames have chunks (17-24 of 23-31 logged). A pass costs ~5 ms of CPU whether it draws a
  12-row band or a 236-row chunk: the layer tree's render, not the area (JIHAD_GPU_PHASES: render layers
  2.5-4 ms a composite, the transaction around it ~1.4).
  The columns are Wikipedia's two pinned panels: sticky painted layers with no CONTENT_OPAQUE flag (the
  contents at x 22..165 rows 157..699, the tools at x 603..738 rows 157..466, r319 JIHAD_GPU_DIFFLOG=1),
  so they are composed again every frame and cut into chunks to fit the 256-row band surface.
  Express frames are serial: post, compose and read, publish; median gap 43-48 ms (r315-r320).

CPU over 4 s of the pan (r316/r317/r318/r320)
  the daemon ~1.5 cores: Compositor 59-80 %, the main thread 33-49 %, JihadExpress 12-15 %
  WebAppMgr (the card's blit of each frame) 17-19 %, sensord 17-18 % and atlas-sensord 2-3 % (the Atlas
  browser's accelerometer bridge; not ours, there all the time), pmsyslogd/powerd/... ~10 %: both cores full.

Tried and not kept
  the shift on a worker thread while the passes compose (r315): band frames 37.9-40.4 ms against 37.8 inline,
    median gap 46 against 43: with both cores full the copy only competes (it took 6-19 ms on the worker)
  no band surface, every frame the whole path (r318, JIHAD_GPU_NO_BAND_TARGET=1): jobs median 67 ms against 41.5
  only a band frame's first pass notifies the main thread (r320): median gap 46-48 either way
  (round 265, T-515: a 600 or 942-row band surface, fewer chunks: median 45/46 ms, no gain)

What is left to try
  one tree traversal for all of a frame's regions (the bands and the chunks rendered inside one transaction,
    the world moved per region): saves the transaction around each extra pass (~1.4 ms each) at least
  see-through sticky columns over content that does not move under them (Wikipedia's panels sit beside the
    article): composed again only when something under them could have changed; needs a rule that cannot
    leave stale pixels on pages with a see-through sticky header over images
  the card invalidating only the changed strip (round 304: +12 % for a canvas animation; nothing for a pan)
  the bench without atlas-sensord's sensord load (18 % of a core): the owner's call

Rounds 325-332: the frame windows (render/browserserver/Src/JihadFrameWindow.h, engine 0053)
  A frame may start further into its shared segment (the segments hold four screens, a frame about
  one): the daemon writes where in a 64-byte trailer at the segment's end before each publish, the
  adapter's rasterBuffer() reads it (only an adapter that marked the segment it made uses it), and a
  slot's frame after a scroll is its window moved by the scroll, not 3.7 MB of rows moved.
  main lane, express off (r325-r327): verify mode after the pan began, Wikipedia 32 frames and the long
    page 38, 0 structurally wrong (without windows, r326: 1); the compositor thread's shift 0-1.3 ms
    against 6.5-8.3, band frames 36.1 / 18.4 ms against 38.0 / 22.8; the main lane's gap is bound
    elsewhere (median 43 against 41)
  express lane on its slot's own frame (r328-r330; it used to copy every frame from the newest slot,
    and dropped the other slots' records on each publish, which both lanes now keep): dumped mid-pan
    frames coherent on both pages; the long page's median gap 26 ms against 29 copying (30.5-36.4 fps);
    Wikipedia no better (42-44 against 43): three frames of entering rows beside the sticky columns'
    chunks overflow the 256-row band surface and 26-52 frames took the whole path against 16-18. The
    lane now takes the faster kind by measured frame time (the other tried every 16th frame).
  default checks of all three interfaces with the window-aware adapters (r332): pass, frames right by eye;
    the cube with the counter beside it: right; a zoom sequence on Wikipedia: 8 of 8 first frames, median 57 ms

Round 333: see-through sticky columns kept once proven still (tried, reverted)
  The rule: a see-through stuck layer's column kept like an opaque one's (its pixels where they were)
  once it composed alike three frames running and the content about to slide under it was one colour,
  composed again every 8th frame. On Wikipedia it turned on for one 13-row sliver only: the contents
  panel's highlight follows the reading position (the panel really changes during a pan) and the tools
  panel's strip below it is not one colour. The per-frame save and compare made frames slower: median
  gap 71-72 ms against 42 (JIHAD_GPU_NO_STILL_COLS=1). What is left on Wikipedia is composing panels that
  change, in 3-4 chunk passes of ~5 ms each: fewer passes (one traversal for all regions) is the open way.

Rounds 342-348: fewer composites a frame, fewer tiles a composite (engine 0055, 0056)
  one-frame regions (r342): a band frame's bands and column chunks composed as regions of one composite
    (the transaction, the diff and BeginFrame/EndFrame once; per region the effective transforms,
    Prepare and RenderLayer): chunked frames compose 16.4-17.0 ms against 22.5 (JIHAD_GPU_PASS_PER_REGION=1),
    the compositor job 35-36.5 against 39.7; verify mode 0 structurally wrong of 32
  the tile cull (r343-r345): a tile whose transformed bounds miss the clip skipped before its texture is
    locked and bound: chunked frames compose 10.6 against 16.9, the long page's 3.9 against 5.9. The first
    version compared against the clip without the render target's origin and lost tiles drawn into an
    intermediate surface (r343: 2 frames of 19 badly wrong); with the origin, verify mode over six runs:
    2 small mismatches in 155 frames, both in the overscan rows above the card's view early in the pan
    (80 and 32 rows), against 0 in 105 without the cull and 1 in 116 in earlier rounds. The cull is the
    compositor's own geometric test made earlier (only a culled tile's lock and bind are skipped), so the
    pixels it draws cannot differ; kept, with JIHAD_GPU_NO_TILE_CULL=1 for A/B.
  desktop Wikipedia's 10 s scripted pan, frames published (r346-r348): 20.6-23.9 fps, the three last runs
    23.4-23.9 (earlier builds 20.0-21.7); typical frame 35-38 ms (median), the mean pulled up by gaps of
    56-180 ms where the express frame waits 26-87 ms in the compositor's queue behind the page's own work
    (tile uploads after its repaints) or the page's script runs 60-212 ms on the other core, and the first
    frames with the second core unplugged
  tried and not kept: a 320/384-row band surface (no change, r347); the express lane on its slot's own
    frame despite the sticky panels (r348, a test switch, pixels wrong there): the compositor job 30.2 ms
