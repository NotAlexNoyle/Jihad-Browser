T-504 (cavekit-gpu-rendering R6): the bounded overview, the owner's design of 2026-10-26
============================================================================================

The case: a double tap in on desktop Wikipedia (0.6882 -> 2.3273, the resolution goes to 2.33), a drag while
zoomed in, a double tap out. The frame kept at the zoom in (T-527, zoomBackPublish) covers only the view as it
was (+-141 overscan rows at the fit zoom); one drag moves the fit view ~190 rows, so nothing was shown until the
paint at the resolution of 1: 532-560 ms after the double tap out (r1, r8). The owner: a half-resolution picture
of a few screens around the zoom-in point, ~4-8 MB per card; a long pan may still wait.

How it works (render/goanna/BrowserPageGoanna.cpp, overviewStep/overviewCovers/overviewPlace/overviewFill;
engine 0069):
- Right after the zoom in's first frame is on the card, while the layer tree still holds the tiles at a
  resolution of 1, the express thread posts two or three "windows": composites of a frame's rows each at the
  kept frame's scroll, a frame below it and a frame above it (those within the page), at half the zoom. All at
  once: the paint at the new resolution can land ~350 ms after the zoom (the card's view alone is painted
  first), and a window posted after its transaction would draw the new tree; posted one at a time, the second
  came after it (r8).
- Engine flag 32 (patch 0069): a window is the scrolled content alone (fixed and stuck layers at shadow opacity
  0) and cleared transparent, so rows no tile covered (past the displayport, past the page) read alpha 0 and are
  left out. Each window is a verify composite: the lanes' next frames see no change from it.
- Each window also returns its fixed and stuck rects (T-544's list) at its own scroll. At the zoom back the
  frame is the overview at twice its size (each row and column between two averaged), with the fixed and stuck
  parts copied from the kept frame to where they sit at the card's new position: where the windows on either
  side of it have them, or, for a part below its place in the kept frame's window and higher in the next one,
  moving with the page until it sticks (a sticky top). Any other case makes no frame (the paint is waited for).
- Memory: the overview 1.8 MB (two windows, the page's top) to 2.75 MB (three), beside the kept frame's 3.7 MB;
  the windows' scratch 5.6-7.5 MB only while they are in flight (~0.5 s after a zoom in).
- Where it is used: a zoom back "under half" (the tiles at the new resolution would be drawn at under half their
  size, the express lane makes no frame from them: a double tap to 2.33 and back). A zoom back from a smaller
  zoom in (1.26 -> 0.69) is composed from the zoomed tiles as before (247 ms in r5).

The review (inspector, read-only) and the revision, 2026-10-26
- F-001 (P1): every readback forced the alpha byte to 0xFF, so the "no tile covered this row" test never fired:
  rows past the old displayport would have reached the card as black (every window above had taken all its rows,
  even those above the page's top). Engine: an overview job's read keeps its alpha (the read thread and the
  compositor thread's read); jihad_offscreen_overview_ok answers 0 with the swizzle pass (its shader forces it).
  After: the window above the page's top takes 539-541 of 612 half rows (v1, v4).
- F-002 (P2): the fixed and stuck parts were copied from the kept frame with what was beneath them at the old scroll
  (a see-through part, 2-4 px of old text around each). Now an engine flag 64 job composes the parts alone at the
  kept frame's scroll and zoom (every leaf layer outside a fixed or stuck one at opacity 0, cleared transparent,
  read with their alpha), and the daemon draws them premultiplied over the picture. Test page
  render/goanna/test/jihad-overview-alpha.html (a see-through fixed header and sticky sidebar beside a numbered
  column): v5, the frame from the overview 41 ms after the double tap out; beneath the header lines 3 and r51-r66
  as on the painted card 4 s later, the sidebar stuck under the header at the same row, line 4 at view row 111 in
  both.
- F-003 (P2): past the outermost window every part was held; now the outermost window and the next one in decide
  (held, moving with the page, or with it until it sticks), else no frame.
- F-004 (P3): the verify guard now keeps the sticky probe's state (a window's scroll step left the next real frame a
  probe of up to 1024 px); the overview's jobs use a fixed probe of 1024 px so every window lists the same parts.
- F-005 (P3): a forgotten job's scratch is freed 10 s later instead of never; the scratch is what the engine writes
  (the parts' frame and each window's half frame: 7.5 MB for two windows, 9.4 for three, ~0.5 s).
- F-006 (P3): parts are matched one to one, the nearest in y.
- Found on the way (v4): a window's last 4 card rows sit at its clip's edge and read uncovered; the windows overlap
  by 32 rows now.
