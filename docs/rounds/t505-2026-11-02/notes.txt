T-505, the long page's wrong verify frame (2026-11-02)
======================================================
The owed item from PICKUP 2026-10-26 (night), open item 2: one frame of 58 in pan-v1-long (2026-10-26) had 13 wrong
rows at frame rows 0..13 (slot 1, y 43, shift 184, own slot, bands 1040..1224).

Reproduced (pan-v2-long: JIHAD_GPU_VERIFY=1 JIHAD_GPU_VERIFY_DUMP_WRONG=1, "pan 0 3000 15000", main lane only, the
daemon f25ef5ba / engine 446b22790a of the device): 81 verified frames, 2 wrong, both slot 1:
- line 581: y -133, shift 8 from its y -141 frame, bands 1216..1224: 8 rows wrong at 133..141 = the base's header
  (part rect 0,140..765,198, opaque rows 141..197) where the shift of 8 left its top rows, not put back. The dump
  (vs-slot.raw / vs-whole.raw, looked at: rows 133..140 are the header's #2b4c7e in the frame, the page's #f8f6f0 in
  the reference; 141..197 the header in both; the out-of-page rows above white in both).
- line 615: y 42, shift 175 from that y -133 frame: 14 rows wrong at 0..14, rows 14..23 right. Exactly the record
  the first wrong frame left behind: its beneath (rows 140..198, captured after its restore failed) holds the old
  header in its rows 1..49 and the page's background in 49..58; shifted by 175 those land at 0..14 (wrong) and
  14..23 (right). So the restore WORKS for a base at y -133 and FAILED for the base at y -141 (the load's frame,
  rows 0..141 above the page); the defect heals itself one frame later.
The same geometry in pan-v1-long (2026-10-26): slot 1's first shift (184) from its y -141 frame, 13 wrong rows at
0..13 = the header's bottom (184..197) not put back.
Not it (read): cpuPartsDress's restore arithmetic (d = frameY - srcY, rows outside the read bands), the own-slot
path's srcSlot/srcY (baseSlot = slot, baseY = the slot's y), the engine's frameY (set only with a src or a
diff-first shift), the published top= (the card's scroll, not the frame's y), the part rect's inflation (the
engine's Inflate(1): 58 rows, "the edges' filtered pixels"), the hide of the parts (CompositeToTarget's hook, both
paths).
The daemon instrumented (cpuPartsDress logs the base record, d, the read rows, the rows put back and a pixel of the
record's second row; the main poll logs the job's window against the slot's) and the round again (pan-v3-long, 84
verified frames, 2 wrong, slot 2 this time: shift 1 from y -141 -> 1 wrong row at 140; then shift 137 -> 56 wrong at
3..59). The log settles it (lines 435, 629, 670): the restore RAN ("58 rows put back"), and the base record's probe
read (100,1)=ff2b4c7e, the header, where the other slots' records read fff8f6f0, the page: the record was made bad at
its own dress (line 435), by a frame whose job was the load's tree change "changed 141..1083 x 764..768" (the 4 px
strip beside the scroll port), too tall for the band surface -> "the whole path".

ROOT CAUSE (the inspector's F1, found the same way from the code, context/impl/impl-review-findings-t505-cpuparts.md
review 2): LayerManagerComposite::UpdateAndRender makes a composite whose change is smaller than the frame a PARTIAL
one, clipped and cleared to the change's bounds (here x 764..768), while a frame without its parts reads the changed
rows WHOLE (review D4 of 0072 turned the column read off for it). The columns beside the change come from the window
target's previous composite: in a verify run the whole reference with the parts -> the header in the frame's rows
141..197 before the dress -> the dress records it as what lies beneath -> the first shift puts it back where the old
header landed. Frames with the engine's parts read the columns (T-511), so they never saw it; band-path reads compose
exactly what they read.
FIX (engine 0073, LayerManagerComposite::JihadWholeRows, set by the job's content-only scope): a frame without its
parts has its diff-driven partial composite cover the changed rows across the frame's width, and reports them so.
With it, the inspector's F2-F6 (one sample time per job; overlapping part rects re-merged in the engine and every
beneath recorded before any part is drawn in the daemon; the main lane's second job only on a base of its kind; the
signature's misses; the parts pass under the job's cancel flag).
