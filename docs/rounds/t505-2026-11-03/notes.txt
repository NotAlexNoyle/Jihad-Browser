What changed
------------
Engine: each rect of a frame's parts list carries a signature of its own (JihadPartsSig: the outermost fixed or
sticky layer's subtree as before, plus the paints under THAT layer — CompositableTransactionParent notes each paint
by its outermost part, sJihadPartPaint — and the rect's size; a rect no part's rect meets takes every part's). The
whole signature is the rects' and the global paint count (the one-signature daemon reads every part, as E2 did).
compose_post3/start3 (overview_ok 4) take the list of signatures the daemon holds; the pass composes and reads only
the rects not in it, packed in rect order, and the poll's 54 ints carry each rect's signature and the rects read.
Daemon: the pictures kept by part (eight), each frame dressed from the picture of each of its rects' signature;
JIHAD_GPU_PARTS_PER=1 sends the engine the signatures held (the whole mode without it: post2 with the whole
signature, every part read when it moved). JIHAD_GPU_EXPRESS_SECOND_PARTS=1: the express lane's second frame on the
first in flight again when both are without their parts; the second's dress puts back what lay beneath the first's
parts AND beneath the first's base's parts (the slot records keep their base's serial and the rows read; a slot's
previous record is kept aside for a frame built on its own slot), so the copy may catch the first's dress at any
point. JIHAD_GPU_VERIFY=1 now verifies the express lane's frames too (the reference composed right before the job,
the compare shared with the main lane: "verify express " / "verify express (two deep) " lines).
