Where the 2.2 s went (double tap in): the hold released at the express frame (114 ms); the resolution and
the displayport with its margins (a height each way, half a width sideways) painted in one go: 685 ms of
layer paint, then 442 ms on the compositor thread for the transaction (219 uploads, 414 new textures); the
main lane's frame was composed at +1367 ms and then waited 845 ms for the main thread, which sat in an
1145 ms pump of the page's script (the scroll handlers after the anchor move) — only the main thread
publishes its own frames, and the express lane serves card moves only.

What changed (daemon):
- the critical displayport: a zoom that changes the resolution paints what the card shows first, no margins
  (GoannaRenderPage::SetGpuDpCritical); the margins once a frame of that paint is out (gpuCriticalEnd, 2 s at
  most). JIHAD_GPU_NO_CRITICAL=1 turns it off.
- the express lane publishes a main-lane frame composed while the main thread is away in the engine (40 ms,
  JIHAD_GPU_EXPRESS_TAKEOVER_MS): it polls the job, publishes the slot the compositor read it into and takes
  the job off the main lane's list; the main lane kicks the express thread when it posts (the thread slept up
  to a second) and the thread looks every 3 ms while a main-lane frame is in flight.
- the hold ends when the express frame at the new zoom is POSTED, not published: the compositor thread
  composes it on the tree as it stands, and the paint at the new resolution runs meanwhile.
- a zoom out past half the painted resolution (2.33 -> 0.69) makes no frame from the old tiles: drawn at 0.296
  they cost 210 draws and 471 ms of the compositor thread for a mostly blank frame (and held the paint's
  transaction behind them); the paint goes first and the card scales its last frame meanwhile.
- JIHAD_GPU_ZOOM_DUMP_SEQ=1: each lane's first three frames at a zoom kept (<path>.<n>.<k>).

What the frame dumps showed (fz1-crit / fz1-nocrit / fz1-cm300 / fz1-base): the first critical frame was

JIHAD_GPU_CRIT_MARGIN=1 (a 1 px margin) the same; 300 px of margin covered the view. Cause, in the engine:

overscan rows below the view are blank until the margins come).

The flick's coast gap (FINGER-TESTS, the GPU path's scroll row)
---------------------------------------------------------------
The card sends its position on every step of a kinetic coast (BrowserAdapter::scrollTo), so the gap was lag: a
600 px flick coasts at ~4000 px/s, the express frame is ~1-2 frame times old when the card shows it, and the card
slides that older frame by the difference (the overscan covers 141 rows; past it, grey; the header, baked into the
frame, slides off). Now the express lane composes each frame at the card's position plus its speed (smoothed over
its positions, the reader thread) times JIHAD_GPU_EXPRESS_PREDICT_MS (80), no further than a card's height, not
above the page's top; renderedY says where the frame is, so the adapter places it. By finger (Enyo, the long page):
the shots 0 and 250 ms after a flick have content to the bottom (were 27 % and 50 % grey); the header still slides
for the first ~250 ms. Slow drags unchanged (the header in place mid-stroke, no grey).
