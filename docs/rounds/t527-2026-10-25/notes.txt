T-527, 2026-10-25 (night): the zoom's sharp frame (cavekit-gpu-rendering R6)
============================================================================

Bench: the TouchPad, CE 3.1.0, the sensors running (the product's condition), the Enyo card on a hand-started
daemon (gpu-spike-start.sh: the GPU path, the express lane, the timeline, JIHAD_GPU_ZOOM_DUMP=log, the shipped
followOverflow), desktop en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HP_TouchPad 20 s after the launch, the emulated finger
(tools/device/t502/finger-zoom.sh): a double tap in on the infobox (0.69 -> 2.33), a double tap out, a pinch in
(-> 1.11) and a pinch out (-> 0.78).

Baseline (head: daemon 47a68434, libxul 161d7a88): fz-r2-wiki-daemon-gpu.log
  ms after the zoom    first frame at the zoom   first frame painted at its resolution
  double tap in        117 (old tiles scaled)    2212
  double tap out       490 (minified, partial)   1890
  pinch in              55                       1660
  pinch out             77                        746
Injected zooms (zoom-seq.sh, 8 in and out): first frames 8/8 within 248 ms (median 125): zoomseq-r1-wiki-base.

Where the 2.2 s went (double tap in): the hold released at the express frame (114 ms); the resolution and
the displayport with its margins (a height each way, half a width sideways) painted in one go: 685 ms of
layer paint, then 442 ms on the compositor thread for the transaction (219 uploads, 414 new textures); the
main lane's frame was composed at +1367 ms and then waited 845 ms for the main thread, which sat in an
1145 ms pump of the page's script (the scroll handlers after the anchor move) — only the main thread
publishes its own frames, and the express lane serves card moves only.

What changed (daemon):
- the critical displayport: a zoom that changes the resolution paints what the card shows first, no margins
  (GoannaRenderPage::SetGpuDpCritical); the margins once a frame of that paint is out (gpuCriticalEnd, 2 s at
  most). JIHAD_GPU_NO_CRITICAL=1 turns it off.
- the express lane publishes a main-lane frame composed while the main thread is away in the engine (40 ms,
  JIHAD_GPU_EXPRESS_TAKEOVER_MS): it polls the job, publishes the slot the compositor read it into and takes
  the job off the main lane's list; the main lane kicks the express thread when it posts (the thread slept up
  to a second) and the thread looks every 3 ms while a main-lane frame is in flight.
- the hold ends when the express frame at the new zoom is POSTED, not published: the compositor thread
  composes it on the tree as it stands, and the paint at the new resolution runs meanwhile.
- a zoom out past half the painted resolution (2.33 -> 0.69) makes no frame from the old tiles: drawn at 0.296
  they cost 210 draws and 471 ms of the compositor thread for a mostly blank frame (and held the paint's
  transaction behind them); the paint goes first and the card scales its last frame meanwhile.
- JIHAD_GPU_ZOOM_DUMP_SEQ=1: each lane's first three frames at a zoom kept (<path>.<n>.<k>).

What the frame dumps showed (fz1-crit / fz1-nocrit / fz1-cm300 / fz1-base): the first critical frame was
sharp but painted only the left 300 card px (the rest of the view white) — the painted edge at 1536 screen px.
JIHAD_GPU_CRIT_MARGIN=1 (a 1 px margin) the same; 300 px of margin covered the view. Cause, in the engine:
nsLayoutUtils::PaintFrame asks the root scroll frame's DecideScrollableLayer with aAllowCreateDisplayPort,
which set the root's displayport base to the composition size at the origin in CSS px on every paint — the
base jihad_layout_set_displayport2 sets (what the card shows at the compositor's shift, in layout device px,
patch 0059) never reached a paint; zoomed in, the displayport covered what the margins and the clamp into the
scrollable rect happened to reach. Engine patch 0062 (63c20e39): on the GPU path the paint keeps the daemon's
base. After it the critical frame covers the view (fz1-base: last drawn column 747 in every row; only 54
overscan rows below the view are blank until the margins come).

After (daemon 786f520c, libxul f25bb166 = 63c20e39 stripped): fz-r8-eng-daemon-gpu.log

  double tap in        105                        593

  pinch in              58                        513
  pinch out             80                        551
The paint itself is now the floor: 300-450 ms of the main thread for what the card shows (the refresh
driver's paint in the pump right after the resolution change, then the layer paint), noisy with the page's
own script; at 2.33 the glyphs of a new size are rasterized.

Tile texture pool (JIHAD_GPU_TEX_POOL=160 against the default 48, fz-r9-pool160): the critical paint's
transaction 43 ms against 136-150 (double tap in), 69 against 177 (double tap out): a resolution change frees
every tile, the pool keeps 48 and deletes the rest, and the new paint creates and converts textures again
(1.5-1.7 ms each). Not changed yet: up to 28 MB more of GPU memory per daemon (R10's three-daemon budget).

Pans on the new engine (no regression): pan-p1-* (express lane, sensors running): the long page 29.5-35.4 fps
(was 30-35), desktop Wikipedia 22.3-28.5 (was 21-22); the mid-pan dumps looked at (the header fixed, the
paragraphs 9 -> 17 -> 25, Wikipedia's panels in place). Verify without the express lane (pan-v2-*): 59 and 69
frames, 0 rows wrong, 0 structurally wrong. The long page's frames now differ below the threshold in most
frames (53 of 59) where T-519 found none; the head binaries do the same (pan-v4-long-old: 54 of 61), so it is
not this change (the larger displayport of T-541 or the shipped followOverflow the run used).
